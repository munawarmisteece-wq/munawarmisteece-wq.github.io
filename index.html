About

Final-year student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), Dhaka, graduating in 2027.

I want green energy to work in everyday life, designed for Bangladesh's real social and economic conditions.

My academic research sits where engineering meets sustainability, in three areas: energy policy, energy trade and renewable energy systems. Preserving our world is, to me, the first step before any future technology.

My philosophy is simple: hard work coupled with continuous learning is the basis of meaningful success. Outside class I chair the IEEE MIST Student Branch, serve as General Secretary of the MIST Robotics Club and Vice President of the MIST Innovation Club.

I'm always looking for new challenges and for colleagues who are working toward a sustainable future.

  • Energy policy

    The plans and rules that decide how a country generates, prices and uses power.

  • Energy trade

    How electricity, fuel and equipment cross borders, and what that dependency means for a country.

  • Renewable energy systems

    Solar, wind and storage, sized for real loads and real budgets.

Also interested in power generation, power plants and substations.

Research

Multidisciplinary research has always been a goal of mine. My first journal paper connects electrical engineering with industrial policy and macroeconomics.

my first journal paper!
Accepted for publication Scientific Reports, Nature Portfolio Q1 journal

A Data-Driven Assessment of Structural Barriers to Electrical Equipment Manufacturing in Bangladesh: Trade Dependency, Industrial Competitiveness, and Policy Pathways

The paper uses data to assess what holds back the manufacture of electrical equipment in Bangladesh. It looks through three lenses: how much the country depends on imports, how competitive local industry is, and which policy pathways could change that.
